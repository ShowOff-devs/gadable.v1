# Public Landing Page Scope

## Objective

Create a simple, professional public introduction to GADable that explains the platform, its human-controlled workflow, the Knowledge Hub, and indicative agency subscription packages.

The initial landing page must not claim that organizations are already participating.

## Navigation

- Home
- About GADable
- How It Works
- Assessment Tools
- Knowledge Hub
- Pricing
- Contact
- User Sign In
- Administrative Access

Administrative Access contains:

- Organization Admin Sign In
- Super Admin Sign In

The Super Admin link is deliberately less prominent than User Sign In.

## Required sections

1. Header and accessible mobile navigation
2. Hero: "Smarter GAD Planning. Stronger Inclusive Outcomes."
3. Platform overview and intended users
4. PAP submission-to-reporting workflow
5. Authorized assessment tools
6. Human-guided AI
7. Five role summaries
8. Knowledge Hub preview
9. Security and governance
10. Subscription pricing
11. Contact and final call to action
12. Footer

## Knowledge Hub

The Knowledge Hub is the public learning and information area. It may later contain:

- Approved public PAP summaries
- Published accomplishment reports
- GAD policies and guidelines
- Manuals and reference materials
- Downloadable templates
- Training materials
- News and announcements
- Good practices and success stories
- Frequently asked questions

Only explicitly approved public content may appear. Never show draft PAPs, evaluator comments, internal scores, personal information, or restricted financial details.

Until APIs and approved content exist, use clearly labeled preview content or a truthful empty state.

Use the peso symbol in the interface when supported. The annual subscription is the primary displayed price; monthly equivalent and implementation fee are supporting information.

Required disclaimer:

> Indicative prices are inclusive of 12% VAT and subject to requirements validation, final scope, contract terms, and applicable procurement rules. Monthly amounts are equivalents only; subscriptions are billed annually.

Use "Request a Demonstration" or "Request a Proposal." Do not add Buy Now, Subscribe Now, checkout, or online payment.

## Package summaries

### Agency Starter

- One organization
- Up to 15 named users
- Up to 3 Evaluators or Approvers
- Up to 500 PAP submissions annually
- 20 GB storage
- 250 AI analyses annually
- Core submission, evaluation, revision, and approval workflow
- Basic assessment templates and reports
- One online training session

### Agency Professional - Recommended

- One organization
- Up to 40 named users
- Up to 10 Evaluators or Approvers
- Up to 3,000 PAP submissions annually
- 100 GB storage
- 1,500 AI analyses annually
- Complete supported assessment templates
- Configurable scorecards and expertise-based assignment
- Multi-stage workflow, monitoring, financial summaries, and advanced analytics
- Up to three training sessions annually

### Agency Premium

- One main institution with up to 10 internal units
- Up to 100 named users
- Up to 25 Evaluators or Approvers
- Up to 10,000 PAP submissions annually
- 300 GB storage
- 5,000 AI analyses annually
- Unit hierarchy and delegated administrators
- Consolidated analytics, cross-unit comparison, and executive dashboards
- Up to six training sessions annually

## Demonstration data

Dashboard previews may show sample PAP status, evaluation progress, approved budget, GAD-attributed amount, and monitoring status only when clearly labeled "Preview" or "Demonstration data."

Do not hardcode or imply real platform statistics.

## Landing-page completion boundary

- Connect sign-in links only to existing safe routes.
- If an authentication or contact backend is not implemented, create only an honest placeholder or disabled state.
- Do not simulate login or contact-form success.
- Do not build dashboards in this phase.
- Do not add Participating Organizations to navigation, content, counters, footer, or logos.
